# Privacy Policy

## Article 1 (Information Collected)
- Email, nickname, platform (Android/iOS), FCM token
- App name, package name, description URL, test email (optional)
- Device identifier (device_id): For tester identification
- App usage data: Screen views, feature usage, etc. (only for tests with Analytics enabled)

## Article 2 (Purpose of Collection)
Information is collected for trust score calculation, private test invitations, notification delivery, and participation matching.
App usage pattern analysis is provided to developers in anonymized form only.

## Article 3 (Retention Period)
Information is retained until account deletion. Feedback content is temporarily stored for one week.

## Article 4 (Third-Party Disclosure)
The App does not provide personal information to third parties except as required by law.

## Article 5 (Outsourcing of Personal Information Processing)
The Company outsources the following personal information processing to external companies:

- **Push notification processing and FCM token management**
  - Contractor: Google FCM (Google LLC, USA)
  - Contact: [support.google.com/fcm](https://support.google.com/fcm)

- **Database hosting and authentication/RLS management**
  - Contractor: Supabase Inc. (USA)
  - Contact: [supabase.com/contact](https://supabase.com/contact)

## Article 6 (User Rights)
Users may request access, correction, or deletion of their information at any time through the app or email.

## Article 7 (Security Measures)
Personal information is protected through HTTPS communication and Supabase RLS.

## Article 10 (Opting Out of Personalized Ads)
You can change ad personalization settings in your device settings:
- **Android**: Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization
- **iOS**: Settings > Privacy > Tracking > Allow Apps to Request to Track (disable)

## Article 11 (App Usage Analysis Service)

1. When participating in a test with Analytics enabled by the developer, your app usage data will be collected.
2. Tester information is anonymized (Tester A, B, etc.) before being provided to developers.
3. Testers receive an additional credit bonus (+10) upon completing Analytics-enabled tests.
4. Upon account deletion, device identifiers are anonymized, and existing analytics data is used for statistical purposes only.
