Privacy Policy

Raise Hell | mkideabox Co., Ltd. | Effective date: October 11, 2026

mkideabox Co., Ltd. (the "Company") values the privacy of users of "Raise Hell" (the "App") and complies with applicable data protection laws, including Korea's Personal Information Protection Act and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Information We Collect

The App has no account registration, and the Company does not directly collect personal information such as your name or contact details.

  • Game progress data: Stored only on your device and never sent to the Company's servers.
  • Advertising information: The App uses Google AdMob rewarded ads. To serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect your advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID / iOS IDFA), IP address, device information, and approximate location. For details, see Google's advertising policies.
  • Ad consent: Users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland are asked through Google's consent form, on first launch, whether they consent to personalized ads. If you do not consent, only non-personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the App under “Stats → Ad privacy options”.

2. How We Use Information

  • Saving your game progress
  • Serving rewarded ads (watched only when you choose to)

3. Third-Party Sharing and Processing

RecipientPurposeData

4. Retention and Deletion

Game data is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App. You can also erase it in the App with “Stats → Reset Data”.

5. Your Rights

  • Reset your advertising identifier or turn off personalized ads: device Settings > Privacy (or Google) > Ads
  • Opt out of tracking on iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking
  • Change ad consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): “Stats → Ad privacy options” in the App

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA where local law requires).
