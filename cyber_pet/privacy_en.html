Cozy Pet Watch Face Privacy Policy

Scope

Applies to Cozy Pet Watch Face 2.0, effective when version 2.0 is publicly released.

Display and settings

The face shows device time, date and your selected friend. When connected to the separate Cozy Pet app through its complication, it displays pet species, outfit and resting state. Wear OS manages face settings. This is a code-free Watch Face Format face with no developer server, advertising, analytics or accounts.

Separate app and removal

Care and purchases belong to the separate Cozy Pet app and its privacy policy. Uninstall the face to stop using it. Wear OS policies govern system settings retention.
