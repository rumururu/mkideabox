Privacy Policy — Cyber Pet

Effective date: September 16, 2026

Cyber Pet (the “watch face”) is a Wear OS watch face published on Google Play by MK Ideabox (the “developer”).

This watch face does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. It has no account, sign-in, internet connection, advertising or analytics.

1. Personal information we collect

None — no name, e-mail address, device identifier, location or health data.

2. How the watch face works

The watch face is built with Google’s Watch Face Format, a declarative format that contains no executable code; the Wear OS system draws the screen itself. It has no internet permission and there is no companion phone app.

3. Steps, battery and heart rate shown on screen

The step count (STEPS/EXP gauge) and battery level (BATTERY/HP gauge) are rendered by the Wear OS system. The watch face never stores these values or sends them off the watch; having no code, it is not able to. Complication values (heart rate by default, plus an optional slot) come from the system or third-party provider apps you select in your watch settings, and their collection and processing is governed by that provider’s privacy policy. You can change or remove any complication at any time in the watch face editor.

4. Working with the Cyber Pet Care companion app

If you install the free Cyber Pet Care app and link it to the pet slot, that app passes your pet’s state (growth stage, fullness, mood, name and level) to the watch face as a complication value inside the same watch. These values never leave the watch and are never delivered to the developer. See the Cyber Pet Care privacy policy.

5. Permissions used

PermissionPurpose
com.google.android.wearable.permission.RECEIVE_COMPLICATION_DATALets the Wear OS system display the complication values you selected. The data stays inside the system and is never delivered to the developer.

No other permission (internet, location, sensors, activity recognition, contacts, etc.) is requested.

6. Information stored on the device

The choices you make in the editor (pet, palette, scene, name plate, preset) are stored on the watch by the Wear OS system only. The developer has no access to them, and they are removed when the watch face is uninstalled.

7. Sharing and international transfer

Since no information is collected, nothing is shared with third parties or transferred abroad.

8. Children’s privacy

The watch face is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone, regardless of age.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date on this page will be updated; any change in data handling will be announced with the app update and in the store listing.
