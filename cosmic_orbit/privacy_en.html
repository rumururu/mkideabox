Privacy Policy — Cosmic Orbit

Effective date: September 13, 2026

Cosmic Orbit (the “watch face”) is a Wear OS watch face published on Google Play by MK Ideabox (the “developer”).

This watch face does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. It has no account, sign-in, internet connection, advertising or analytics.

1. Personal information we collect

None. The developer does not collect names, email addresses, device identifiers, location, health information or any other personal information.

2. How the watch face works

The watch face is built with Google’s Watch Face Format, a declarative format with no executable app code. Wear OS draws the screen. The watch face has no internet permission and no companion phone app.

3. Complications

Values such as battery level, heart rate and step count come from the system or third-party provider app selected by the user and are displayed by Wear OS. The watch face does not store those values or send them off the watch. Each provider’s privacy policy governs its own processing. Users can change or remove complications at any time in the watch face editor.

4. Permission used

com.google.android.wearable.permission.RECEIVE_COMPLICATION_DATA lets Wear OS display the complication values selected by the user. The data is not delivered to the developer. No internet, location, sensor, contacts or other permission is requested.

5. Information stored on the device

Editor choices such as dial, accent color and preset are stored only on the watch by Wear OS. The developer cannot access them. They are removed when the watch face is uninstalled.

6. Sharing and international transfer

Because no information is collected, no information is shared with third parties or transferred internationally.

7. Children’s privacy

The watch face is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone, regardless of age.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date on this page will be updated. Any change to data handling will be announced with an app update and in the store listing.
