Privacy Policy — LITTLE SEASONS (Apple Watch)

Effective date: November 14, 2026

LITTLE SEASONS (the “app”) is an Apple Watch app and widget published on the App Store by MK Ideabox (the “developer”). The policy for the Google Play watch face is on a separate page.

The developer does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

1. Personal information we collect

None. The developer receives no information — no name, e-mail address, device identifier, location or health data — and runs no servers.

2. Location and weather

To draw the current weather (clouds, rain, snow and so on) in the scene, the app uses your Apple Watch's approximate location, only if you allow it. The location is used on the watch only to request the weather from Apple's weather service (WeatherKit); it is never sent to the developer and is not recorded. Apple handles weather requests under Apple's privacy policy. If you don't allow location access, the app still works and draws a clear sky.

3. Permissions

The app asks only for location (while using the app, approximate). You can turn it off at any time in Apple Watch Settings.

4. Information stored on the device

The last weather received (condition, temperature and time) and the scene you choose for a widget are stored on the watch only. The developer cannot access them, and they are removed when you delete the app.

5. Sharing and international transfer

Because the developer collects nothing, nothing is shared with third parties or transferred abroad. Weather requests go directly from your Apple Watch to Apple.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone, whatever their age.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date on this page will be updated. Any change to how data is handled will be announced in an app update and the store description.
