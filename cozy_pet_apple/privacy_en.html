Cozy Pet for Apple — Privacy Policy

Scope

This policy applies to version 1.0 of Cozy Pet for iPhone and Apple Watch, operated by MK Ideabox Co., Ltd. It takes effect on the first public release of the Apple version.

Information stored on your devices

Your selected pet, outfit, care status, bond and play history are stored locally to provide the game and preserve progress. The app uses no account registration, advertising, tracking, analytics SDK or developer server.

Paired devices and complications

A paired iPhone and Apple Watch exchange game records using Apple WatchConnectivity. The Watch app and its complication share a small snapshot of the pet and care status in a shared container on the same watch. The developer does not receive this information.

Permissions and purchases

The app does not access your camera, microphone, photos, location, contacts or health data. The current Apple version has no in-app purchases.

Retention and deletion

Records remain on each device. To remove all copies, delete the app from both your iPhone and Apple Watch. No developer cloud backup or Android connection is provided. Apple system backups, diagnostics and app distribution are subject to your Apple settings and Apple policies.
