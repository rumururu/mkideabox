1. 요약

케이크 판정단(이하 "앱")은 회원가입이 없고 이름·이메일·전화번호 같은 개인을 식별하는 정보를 수집하지 않습니다. 케이크 사진은 기기 안에서만 처리하며 서버로 보내지 않습니다. 기기 밖으로 정보가 나가는 경우는 다음과 같습니다. (1) 사용자가 동의하고 직접 올린 '오늘의 케이크' 순위 기록(무작위 설치 ID·그림 별명·점수), (2) 사용자가 직접 고른 앱으로 점수 카드를 공유할 때. 또한 앱은 Google AdMob 광고를 보여 주며, 이때 Google이 광고 식별자 등 기기 정보를 처리합니다(아래 '광고'). (3) 사용자가 동의하고 '사진 도전'을 보낼 때의 줄인 케이크 사진과 케이크 모양(아래 '내 사진 도전 링크').

2. 사진과 카메라

카메라와 사진 보관함은 케이크 사진을 찍거나 고를 때만 사용합니다. 사진, 테두리·칼선·토핑 표시, 채점은 모두 기기 안에서 처리하며 개발자 서버나 제3자에게 보내지 않습니다. 앱은 사진을 따로 보관하지 않고 화면을 닫으면 메모리에서 지웁니다. 운영체제가 사진을 고르는 과정에서 만든 임시 파일은 앱 캐시에 남을 수 있으며 앱을 삭제하면 함께 지워집니다. 단, 사용자가 직접 '사진 도전'을 보내는 경우는 아래 '내 사진 도전 링크'를 따릅니다.

3. 공유와 저장

'공유하기'를 누르면 점수 카드 이미지(케이크 사진과 점수 포함)를 만들어 운영체제의 공유 화면으로 넘깁니다. 어느 앱(인스타그램, 메신저 등)으로 보낼지는 사용자가 고르며, 개발자는 공유한 내용을 받지 않습니다. 받는 앱에서의 처리는 그 앱의 개인정보처리방침을 따릅니다. 공유 화면에서 '이미지 저장'을 고르면 사진 앱에 저장하며, 이때만 사진 추가 권한을 요청합니다.

4. 내 사진 도전 링크

내 케이크 사진으로 채점한 뒤 공유할 때 처음 한 번 동의를 받으면, 친구가 같은 케이크를 잘라 볼 수 있도록 사진을 긴 쪽 720픽셀로 줄인 JPEG 이미지와 케이크 테두리·토핑 위치를 서버에 올립니다. 이름·계정·기기 ID·위치 정보는 올리지 않으며, 사진의 촬영 정보(EXIF)도 담기지 않습니다. 도전에는 추측하기 어려운 무작위 주소가 붙고, 그 링크를 가진 사람은 누구나 사진을 볼 수 있습니다. 서버는 Cloudflare, Inc.의 Workers·KV 서비스를 사용하며, 남용을 막기 위해 요청한 IP 주소로 1분당 올리기 횟수를 제한합니다(개발자는 IP 주소를 저장하지 않습니다). 도전은 30일 뒤 자동으로 지워집니다. 더 일찍 지우려면 도전 링크를 아래 메일로 보내 주세요. 동의하지 않으면 사진은 올리지 않고 점수만 공유합니다. Privacy Policy · English

Effective date: 2026-11-10

1. Summary

Cake Cut Challenge (the "app") has no sign-up and does not collect information that identifies you, such as your name, email or phone number. Cake photos are processed only on your device and are never sent to a server. Information leaves your device in these cases: (1) "Today's cake" ranking records you choose to post after agreeing (a random install ID, a picture name and your score), and (2) when you share a score card to an app you pick. The app also shows Google AdMob ads, and Google processes device information such as advertising identifiers for them (see "Ads" below). (3) when you agree to send a 'photo challenge', a reduced copy of your cake photo and the cake shape (see 'Photo challenge links' below).

2. Photos and camera

The camera and photo library are used only to take or pick a cake photo. The photo, the edge, cut and topping marks, and the scoring are all handled on your device and are never sent to the developer's server or to third parties. The app does not keep your photos; they are cleared from memory when you leave the screen. Temporary files the operating system creates while you pick a photo may stay in the app's cache and are removed when you delete the app. The only exception is when you choose to send a 'photo challenge' (see 'Photo challenge links' below).

3. Sharing and saving

When you tap "Share", the app creates a score card image (including the cake photo and score) and hands it to your operating system's share screen. You choose which app to send it to (Instagram, messengers, etc.); the developer does not receive what you share. The receiving app's privacy policy applies there. If you choose "Save Image" on the share screen, the image is saved to your Photos, and only then does the app ask for permission to add photos.

4. Photo challenge links

When you share after scoring your own cake photo and agree once, a JPEG copy of the photo reduced to 720 pixels on the long side and the positions of the cake edge and toppings are uploaded so friends can cut the same cake. No name, account, device ID or location is uploaded, and the photo carries no camera metadata (EXIF). Each challenge gets a random address that is hard to guess, and anyone with the link can see the photo. The server runs on Cloudflare, Inc.'s Workers and KV services; to prevent abuse, the requesting IP address is used to limit uploads per minute (the developer does not store IP addresses). Challenges are deleted automatically after 30 days. To delete one sooner, send the challenge link to the email below. If you do not agree, no photo is uploaded and only your score is shared. Cloudflare privacy policy: https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/

5. Information stored on your device

The app stores a random install ID for the rankings, your picture name, and whether you agreed to join the rankings on your device. Player names typed in Battle mode stay in memory only during the battle; they are not saved or sent.

6. Today's cake rankings

The rankings are optional. The first time you tap "Post to rankings", the app asks for your consent and sends information only if you agree. It sends only a random install ID created by the app (not an advertising ID or device ID), a picture name (a dessert picture number and 4 digits, for example 🍰 #4821), the date, the cake type and your score. Photos, cuts, names and anything you type are never sent, and there is no chat. Other users see only the picture name, score and rank. The server runs on Supabase, Inc. (Seoul region) and is used only to show the rankings and prevent abuse (rate limits). Rate-limit records are deleted after one minute; ranking records are kept until you delete them. Supabase privacy policy: https://supabase.com/privacy

7. Deleting your data

Tap "Delete my ranking data" on the rankings screen to delete your ranking records (install ID, picture name, scores) from the server right away; this also withdraws your consent. Deleting the app removes all information stored on your device. If you already deleted the app, email us your picture name and the dates you posted at the address below, and we will delete the records after checking. Ad-related data is handled by Google; you can block personalized ads by resetting or deleting the advertising ID in your device settings (Android) or turning off tracking (iOS). Photo challenges are deleted automatically after 30 days; to delete one sooner, email us the challenge link.

8. Ads (Google AdMob)

The app shows full-screen ads from Google AdMob only right before a score result, and at most once every three results after the first two. Photos and scoring details are never sent to ad companies. AdMob may collect and use advertising identifiers (the Android advertising ID, and the IDFA on iOS if you allow tracking), approximate location estimated from your IP address, device and app information, ad interactions and diagnostic information to serve ads, personalize ads where allowed, measure ad performance and prevent fraud. On iOS the app asks on first launch whether to allow tracking; if you don't, you can still use the app and see non-personalized ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the app shows an ad consent message on first launch, and you can change or withdraw consent anytime with "Ad privacy settings" on the home screen. The app is not directed to children under 13 and limits ad content to a PG rating. Ad removal is sold as an in-app purchase through Google Play and the App Store. Payment is handled by the store and the developer never receives card or other payment details. Whether you bought it is stored on your device. No separate analytics tools are used. Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy , how Google uses data from partner sites: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

9. Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Ranking names are made only from fixed pictures and numbers; there is no free text or chat. If you believe a child has provided information, contact mkideabox.feedback@gmail.com and we will delete it after checking.

1. Ringkasan

Cake Cut Challenge ("aplikasi") tidak memerlukan pendaftaran dan tidak mengumpulkan informasi yang mengidentifikasi Anda, seperti nama, email, atau nomor telepon. Foto kue hanya diproses di perangkat Anda dan tidak pernah dikirim ke server. Informasi keluar dari perangkat Anda dalam hal berikut: (1) catatan peringkat "Kue hari ini" yang Anda kirim sendiri setelah menyetujui (ID instalasi acak, nama bergambar, dan skor), dan (2) saat Anda membagikan kartu skor ke aplikasi pilihan Anda. Aplikasi juga menampilkan iklan Google AdMob, dan Google memproses informasi perangkat seperti ID iklan untuk itu (lihat "Iklan" di bawah). (3) saat Anda setuju mengirim 'tantangan foto', salinan kecil foto kue dan bentuk kue (lihat 'Tautan tantangan foto' di bawah).

2. Foto dan kamera

Kamera dan galeri foto hanya dipakai untuk memotret atau memilih foto kue. Foto, tanda tepi, potongan, dan topping, serta penilaian semuanya diproses di perangkat Anda dan tidak pernah dikirim ke server pengembang atau pihak ketiga. Aplikasi tidak menyimpan foto Anda; foto dihapus dari memori saat Anda keluar dari layar. File sementara yang dibuat sistem saat memilih foto dapat tersisa di cache aplikasi dan terhapus saat aplikasi dihapus. Satu-satunya pengecualian adalah saat Anda sendiri memilih mengirim 'tantangan foto' (lihat 'Tautan tantangan foto' di bawah).

3. Berbagi dan menyimpan

Saat Anda mengetuk "Bagikan", aplikasi membuat gambar kartu skor (berisi foto kue dan skor) lalu menyerahkannya ke layar berbagi sistem. Anda memilih aplikasi tujuan (Instagram, aplikasi pesan, dll.); pengembang tidak menerima apa yang Anda bagikan. Di aplikasi penerima berlaku kebijakan privasi aplikasi tersebut. Jika Anda memilih "Simpan Gambar", gambar disimpan ke Foto, dan hanya saat itu aplikasi meminta izin menambahkan foto.

4. Tautan tantangan foto

Saat Anda membagikan hasil setelah menilai foto kue sendiri dan setuju sekali, salinan JPEG foto yang diperkecil menjadi 720 piksel di sisi panjang serta posisi pinggiran kue dan topping diunggah agar teman bisa memotong kue yang sama. Nama, akun, ID perangkat, atau lokasi tidak diunggah, dan foto tidak memuat data kamera (EXIF). Setiap tantangan mendapat alamat acak yang sulit ditebak, dan siapa pun yang punya tautan bisa melihat fotonya. Server memakai layanan Workers dan KV dari Cloudflare, Inc.; untuk mencegah penyalahgunaan, alamat IP pengirim dipakai untuk membatasi unggahan per menit (pengembang tidak menyimpan alamat IP). Tantangan dihapus otomatis setelah 30 hari. Untuk menghapus lebih cepat, kirim tautan tantangan ke email di bawah. Jika Anda tidak setuju, foto tidak diunggah dan hanya skor yang dibagikan. Kebijakan privasi Cloudflare: https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/

5. Informasi yang disimpan di perangkat

Aplikasi menyimpan di perangkat Anda ID instalasi acak untuk peringkat, nama bergambar Anda, dan apakah Anda setuju ikut peringkat. Nama pemain yang diketik di mode Duel hanya ada di memori selama duel; tidak disimpan atau dikirim.

6. Peringkat Kue hari ini

Peringkat bersifat opsional. Saat pertama kali Anda mengetuk "Kirim ke peringkat", aplikasi meminta persetujuan dan hanya mengirim informasi jika Anda setuju. Yang dikirim hanya ID instalasi acak buatan aplikasi (bukan ID iklan atau ID perangkat), nama bergambar (nomor gambar makanan penutup dan 4 angka, misalnya 🍰 #4821), tanggal, jenis kue, dan skor Anda. Foto, potongan, nama, dan teks yang Anda ketik tidak pernah dikirim, dan tidak ada fitur obrolan. Pengguna lain hanya melihat nama bergambar, skor, dan peringkat. Server berjalan di Supabase, Inc. (wilayah Seoul) dan hanya dipakai untuk menampilkan peringkat dan mencegah penyalahgunaan (pembatasan permintaan). Catatan pembatasan permintaan dihapus setelah satu menit; catatan peringkat disimpan sampai Anda menghapusnya. Kebijakan privasi Supabase: https://supabase.com/privacy

7. Menghapus data Anda

Ketuk "Hapus data peringkatku" di layar peringkat untuk langsung menghapus catatan Anda dari server (ID instalasi, nama bergambar, skor); persetujuan Anda juga dicabut. Menghapus aplikasi menghapus semua informasi yang tersimpan di perangkat. Jika Anda sudah menghapus aplikasi, kirim email ke alamat di bawah berisi nama bergambar dan tanggal Anda mengirim skor; kami akan menghapusnya setelah memeriksa. Data terkait iklan diproses oleh Google; Anda dapat memblokir iklan yang dipersonalisasi dengan mengatur ulang atau menghapus ID iklan di pengaturan perangkat (Android) atau mematikan pelacakan (iOS). Tantangan foto dihapus otomatis setelah 30 hari; untuk menghapus lebih cepat, kirimkan tautan tantangan lewat email.

8. Iklan (Google AdMob)

Aplikasi menampilkan iklan layar penuh Google AdMob hanya tepat sebelum hasil penilaian, dan setelah dua hasil pertama paling banyak sekali setiap tiga hasil. Foto dan detail penilaian tidak pernah dikirim ke perusahaan iklan. AdMob dapat mengumpulkan dan memakai ID iklan (ID iklan Android, dan IDFA di iOS jika Anda mengizinkan pelacakan), perkiraan lokasi dari alamat IP Anda, informasi perangkat dan aplikasi, interaksi iklan, serta informasi diagnostik untuk menayangkan iklan, mempersonalisasikannya bila diizinkan, mengukur kinerja iklan, dan mencegah penipuan. Di iOS, aplikasi menanyakan izin pelacakan saat pertama dibuka; jika tidak diizinkan, Anda tetap bisa memakai aplikasi dan melihat iklan non-personal. Di Wilayah Ekonomi Eropa, Inggris, dan Swiss, aplikasi menampilkan pesan persetujuan iklan saat pertama dibuka, dan Anda dapat mengubah atau mencabut persetujuan kapan saja lewat "Privasi iklan" di layar utama. Aplikasi tidak ditujukan untuk anak di bawah 13 tahun dan membatasi konten iklan pada rating PG. Penghapusan iklan dijual sebagai pembelian dalam aplikasi lewat Google Play dan App Store. Pembayaran diproses oleh toko dan pengembang tidak pernah menerima data kartu atau data pembayaran lain. Status pembelian disimpan di perangkat. Tidak ada alat analitik lain yang digunakan. Kebijakan privasi Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy , cara Google memakai data dari situs mitra: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

9. Privasi anak

Aplikasi ini tidak ditujukan untuk anak di bawah 13 tahun, dan kami tidak dengan sengaja mengumpulkan informasi pribadi anak di bawah 13 tahun. Nama peringkat hanya dibuat dari gambar dan angka yang sudah ditentukan; tidak ada teks bebas atau obrolan. Jika Anda yakin seorang anak telah memberikan informasi, hubungi mkideabox.feedback@gmail.com dan kami akan menghapusnya setelah memeriksa.
