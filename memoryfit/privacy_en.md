# Privacy Policy

**MemoryFit (FSRS-based memory training app)**  
Last updated: May 6, 2026

MemoryFit collects and processes data needed to provide spaced repetition study, card/deck management, optional AI card generation, optional AI image generation, purchases, backup, and account features.

AI requests are processed through MemoryFit servers using Supabase Edge Functions and then sent to Google Gemini API only when users choose an AI generation feature. Users can review AI data use in the app at Settings > AI Settings > AI Data Use.
