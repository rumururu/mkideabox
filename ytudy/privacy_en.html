Privacy Policy

UTudy (hereinafter referred to as "the App") values your personal information and is committed to protecting your privacy in compliance with applicable laws and regulations. This Privacy Policy explains how your personal information is collected, used, and protected.

1. Information We Collect

The App collects the following personal information for registration and service provision:

  • Required Information: Nickname, Email Address
  • Automatically Collected Information: Service usage records, access logs, device ID, advertising identifiers (ADID/IDFA)
  • Location Information (with optional consent)

2. How We Collect Information

The App collects personal information in the following ways:

  • Information entered directly by users during registration
  • Information automatically collected during service use
  • Information collected through advertising networks such as Google AdMob

3. Purpose of Collecting Personal Information

The collected personal information is used for the following purposes:

  • Account Management: Registration, personal identification, prevention of misuse
  • Service Provision: YouTube lecture notifications, note saving, and community features
  • Advertising: Personalized advertising and advertising performance analysis
  • App Improvement: Statistics and analysis to improve user experience

4. Retention and Use Period

The App retains your personal information for the period necessary to fulfill the purposes stated at the time of collection and promptly destroys it thereafter. However, exceptions apply when retention is required by applicable laws.

  • Account Deletion: Immediate deletion
  • Legal Requirements: Retained for the period specified by applicable laws

5. Sharing and Disclosure

The App does not provide or share your personal information with third parties. However, exceptions apply in the following cases:

  • When users have given prior consent
  • When required by law
  • When necessary for advertising through Google AdMob, etc.

5-1. Outsourced Processing

The App outsources personal information processing for service provision and operation as follows:

  • Provider: Supabase Inc.
  • Services: User authentication, database storage, file storage
  • Location: Seoul, Republic of Korea

The Company complies with personal information protection laws when entering outsourcing contracts and supervises the outsourced party to ensure safe processing of personal information.

6. Protection of Personal Information

The App takes the following measures to safely protect your personal information:

  • Encryption of personal information for storage and management
  • Security systems to prevent hacking and external intrusion
  • Limiting access to personal information to minimal personnel

7. User Rights

Users may request access, correction, or deletion of their personal information at any time, and the App will take action without delay. Users also have the right to withdraw consent to the processing of personal information.

8. Cookies and Advertising Identifiers

The App uses cookies and advertising identifiers (ADID/IDFA) to provide personalized services. Users can disable these through device settings.

  • Android: Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization
  • iOS: Settings > Privacy > Advertising > Limit Ad Tracking

9. Data Protection Officer

The App has designated a person responsible for personal information protection. If you have any questions regarding personal information, please contact us:

  • Data Protection Officer: Rose
  • Email: mkideabox@gmail.com

10. Changes to Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be changed according to applicable laws and company policies, and any changes will be announced through the App.

11. YouTube API Usage Disclosure

The App uses YouTube API services to provide YouTube video streaming services to users. Through this, users can access various YouTube content, and the App complies with YouTube's Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy in this process.

12. YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy Links

By watching YouTube videos through the App, users are automatically deemed to agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy. Users can click the above links to review the respective terms and policies.

13. User Data Access and Deletion Rights

Users can visit the Google Security Settings page to manage or revoke access to their data at any time. The App supports users in controlling their own information.
