Privacy Policy — LIQUID TIME

Effective date: November 14, 2026

LIQUID TIME (the “watch face”) is a Wear OS watch face published on Google Play by MK Ideabox (the “developer”).

This watch face does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no internet connection, no advertising and no analytics.

1. Personal information we collect

None. The developer does not collect any information — no name, e-mail address, device identifier, location or health data.

2. How the watch face works

The watch face is built with Google's Watch Face Format, a declarative file format that contains no executable code; Wear OS draws the screen itself. It has no internet permission and no companion phone app. The date and time on screen are drawn by the watch system and are not recorded. The melting digit animation is drawn on the watch screen from the current time only, and is not stored.

3. Permissions

The watch face requests no permissions. The line at the bottom of the screen is a complication slot: Wear OS takes the value from the provider you choose, such as step count (the default) or your next event, and draws it on the watch screen. That value is never sent to the developer and is not recorded. If a provider needs a permission, Wear OS asks for it on behalf of that provider.

4. Information stored on the device

The metal and complication you choose in the editor are stored by Wear OS on the watch only. The developer cannot access them, and they are removed when you uninstall the watch face.

5. Sharing and international transfer

Because nothing is collected, nothing is shared with third parties or transferred abroad.

6. Children

The watch face is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone, whatever their age.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date on this page will be updated. Any change to how data is handled will be announced in an app update and the store description.
