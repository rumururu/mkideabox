Privacy Policy — Cyber HUD

Effective date: September 16, 2026

Cyber HUD (the “watch face”) is a Wear OS watch face published on Google Play by MK Ideabox (the “developer”).

This watch face does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. It has no account, sign-in, internet connection, advertising or analytics.

1. Personal information we collect

None. The developer does not collect any information — no name, e-mail address, device identifier, location or health data.

2. How the watch face works

The watch face is built with Google's Watch Face Format, a declarative format that contains no executable code; the Wear OS system draws the screen itself. It has no internet permission and there is no companion phone app.

3. Complications

The watch face has four complication slots: the left gauge (heart rate by default), the right gauge (step count by default) and two small slots at the top. The values come from the system or third-party provider apps you select and are rendered by the Wear OS system. The watch face never stores them or sends them off the watch. Battery level, charging state and the date and time are drawn by the watch system and are not recorded. Collection and processing of each value is governed by the privacy policy of the provider app, and you can change or remove any complication at any time in the watch face editor.

4. Permissions used

com.google.android.wearable.permission.RECEIVE_COMPLICATION_DATA lets the Wear OS system display the complication values you selected. The data is never delivered to the developer. No other permission (internet, location, sensors, contacts, etc.) is requested.

5. Information stored on the device

The choices you make in the editor (HUD palette, background pattern, radar, seconds, bezel, preset and so on) are stored on the watch by the Wear OS system only. The developer has no access to them, and they are removed when the watch face is uninstalled.

6. Sharing and international transfer

Since no information is collected, nothing is shared with third parties or transferred abroad.

7. Children's privacy

The watch face is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone, regardless of age.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date on this page will be updated. Any change to how data is handled will be announced with the app update and in the store listing.
