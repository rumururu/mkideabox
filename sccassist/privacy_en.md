# Privacy Policy

**SCCAssist (Lane Keeping Assist Monitor)**

## Article 1 (Information Collected)

- Device identifier (anonymized Device ID)
- Camera footage (processed in real-time, not stored)
- HUD sample images (optional, collected only with user consent)

## Article 2 (Purpose of Collection)

- Vehicle HUD status monitoring and notifications
- AI model training improvement (when sample upload is consented)
- App improvement and error analysis

## Article 3 (Retention Period)

- Camera footage: Immediately deleted after real-time processing (not stored)
- Sample images: Deleted within 90 days after model training completion
- Device identifier: Until app deletion

## Article 4 (Third-Party Disclosure)

This app does not provide personal information to third parties except as required by law.

## Article 5 (Data Processing Delegation)

- **Database hosting and model storage**: Supabase Inc. (USA)

## Article 6 (Camera Usage Notice)

- This app uses the camera to monitor vehicle HUD status in real-time.
- Camera footage is processed only on the device and is not transmitted externally.
- However, if you consent to sample upload, anonymized HUD images may be sent to the server.

## Article 7 (User Rights)

Users may request access, correction, or deletion of their data at any time through app settings or email.

## Article 8 (Security Measures)

Data is securely protected through HTTPS communication and Supabase RLS.
